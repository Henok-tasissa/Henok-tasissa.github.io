Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 October 2026

This policy covers the Wordsprout word games published by Henok Tasissa: Wordsprout (English), ቃል በቃል (Amharic) and Palabra a Palabra (Spanish), on iOS and Android. We call them "the app" below.

In short: the app has no accounts and no login. Your game progress stays on your device. The only personal data that leaves your device is what Google AdMob needs to show ads and what Apple, Google Play and RevenueCat need to process purchases. We never sell your data.

1. Data stored on your device

The app saves your progress locally using your device's app storage: the level you are on, coins, stars, your daily streak, collected letter tiles, achievements, settings such as the sound toggle, and whether you have bought Remove Ads. This data is not sent to us. Deleting the app deletes it.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows ads through Google AdMob, including rewarded videos you choose to watch and occasional interstitial ads between levels. To serve and measure ads, Google's SDK may collect:

  • your device's advertising identifier (IDFA on iOS, Advertising ID on Android), only if you allow it
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it
  • device and app information (model, operating system, language, app version)
  • ad interaction data (which ads were shown, viewed or tapped)

How Google uses this data is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Your choices.

  • On iOS, the app asks "Allow tracking?" before using the advertising identifier. If you decline, you still get the whole game and ads become non-personalized.
  • In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, a consent form appears on first launch. You can change your choice at any time from the app's settings, or by reinstalling.
  • You can reset or limit your advertising identifier in your device settings (iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking; Android: Settings → Google → Ads).
  • Buying Remove Ads stops interstitial ads permanently. Rewarded videos remain optional.

3. In-app purchases

Purchases (coin packs, the Starter Pack and Remove Ads) are processed by the Apple App Store or Google Play under their own terms and privacy policies. We never see your payment details.

We use RevenueCat to validate purchases and let you restore them on a new device. RevenueCat receives a random anonymous app user ID generated on your device, the purchase receipt, and basic device information. It does not receive your name or email.

4. What we do not collect

  • No account, name, email, phone number or password
  • No contacts, photos, microphone, camera or precise location
  • No analytics SDK other than what AdMob needs to serve ads

5. Children

The app is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

6. Data retention and deletion

Progress stays on your device until you delete the app. Advertising data is retained by Google under its retention policies. Purchase records are retained by Apple, Google and RevenueCat as long as needed to honor your purchases and meet legal obligations. To ask for deletion of data held by RevenueCat on your behalf, email us with the approximate purchase date and the store you used.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in Europe or the CCPA in California), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or restrict the processing of your personal data, and to object to or withdraw consent for personalized advertising. Use the choices in section 2, or contact us at the address below.

8. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new "Last updated" date.
